# Note-Taking Apps - Notion or Obsidian?

Notion and Obsidian are two very powerful note-taking apps (even if they are not just note-taking apps in the true sense). Today I will discuss their respective advantages, disadvantages and applicability, and see if Obsidian can replace Notion.

## Introduction

If you work in a team and team collaboration is an important part of your work, or you need to manage a large and complex project through a note-taking app, or you need to use it across platforms frequently, then Notion is definitely for you. Kanban board, comments, notifications, and the ease of note sharing make Notion stand out in the above situations. But if you care more about note compatibility (portability), privacy, more customization options, or offline note taking, then Obsidian is more suitable for you. Obsidian's notes are stored locally and are very private. One difference between Obsidian and Notion is that Obsidian provides a variety of community themes to change the look and feel of the app. At the same time, Obsidian also provides some plug-ins to increase the degree of customization.

## My Suggestion

I currently use both Notion and Obsidian. I used to think that using just one app to record everything would help me stay focused and productive, but that's not the case. Developers create these products to solve problems. Just as no one is perfect, neither are products. Both Notion and Obsidian have their own strengths and weaknesses. So, rather than abandoning either one, I choose to use both.

## Notion

Notion needs no introduction; it's much more famous than Obsidian. You can see tons of videos about it on YouTube, giving you the impression that all influencers are using Notion.

Notion is very flexible and has a lot of features.

According to Notion's official website, this app can be used as a note-taking app and can also be used for text editing. These two functions make Notion a good alternative to Evernote and Google Docs. Notion can also be used for project management and can replace Trello, Jira & Asana, thousands of teams around the world use Notion as a tool. Notion can also be used to write Wikis , which replaces Atlassian's Confluence and GitHub Wikis.
