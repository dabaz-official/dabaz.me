At 1 a.m. Beijing time on June 6, the WWDC23 keynote began. Here are 14 highlights.

---

## 1. NameDrop

<PostFigure
  src={figure1}
  alt="Two iPhones brought together to exchange contact details"
  caption="NameDrop with iPhone"
/>

With iOS 17, you can share contact information by bringing your iPhone close to someone else's. You can do the same between an iPhone and an Apple Watch.

<PostFigure
  src={figure2}
  alt="An iPhone and an Apple Watch brought together to share contact details"
  caption="NameDrop with iPhone and Apple Watch"
/>

## 2. Contact Poster

iOS 17 lets you design your own Contact Poster, which appears when you call someone.

<PostFigure
  src={figure3}
  alt="A custom Contact Poster"
  caption="Contact Poster"
/>

## 3. FaceTime message

When someone does not answer your FaceTime call, you can leave a FaceTime message, much like the old voicemail.

## 4. FaceTime on Apple TV

FaceTime is coming to Apple TV.

<PostFigure
  src={figure4}
  alt="A FaceTime call on Apple TV"
  caption="FaceTime with Apple TV"
/>

## 5. Stickers from your own photos

You can turn your own photos into stickers in iMessage.

<PostFigure
  src={figure5}
  alt="A custom sticker cut out of a photo of a person"
  caption="Customized Stickers"
/>

## 6. Journal, a new app

Journal is a new app. It lets you bring together photos, music, location, even fitness data to remember a stretch of time.

<PostFigure
  src={figure6}
  alt="A Journal entry mixing photos and text"
  caption="Journal"
/>

## 7. Check In

Check In automatically lets your friends know when you arrive somewhere.

## 8. Adaptive Audio

You no longer have to fiddle with the audio settings on AirPods Pro or other audio devices. With Adaptive Audio, if you run into someone and they start talking to you, the AirPods switch to Transparency mode. If they detect noise, they switch to noise cancellation.

<PostFigure
  src={figure7}
  alt="The Adaptive Audio control for AirPods Pro"
  caption="Adaptive Audio"
/>

## 9. Multiple timers and widgets

You can now set multiple timers across all your Apple devices, and add widgets to Apple Watch that appear with a swipe.

## 10. 15-inch MacBook Air with M2

The 15-inch MacBook Air with the M2 chip is coming, and it weighs only about 1.5 kg.

<PostFigure
  src={figure8}
  alt="The 13-inch and 15-inch MacBook Air with M2 side by side"
  caption="The 13-inch and 15-inch MacBook Air with M2"
/>

## 11. StandBy

StandBy makes your iPhone useful when you are not using it.

<PostFigure
  src={figure9}
  alt="The calendar shown in StandBy"
  caption="Calendar in StandBy"
/>

## 12. Voicemail transcription

Voicemail is transcribed in real time.

<PostFigure
  src={figure10}
  alt="A voicemail being transcribed into text as it is left"
  caption="Voicemail transcribed in real time"
/>

## 13. Log your mood

You can now log how you feel in the Health app across your Apple devices.
