## A closer look

### Apple Intelligence, a new kind of AI

Apple Intelligence is a personal intelligence system built into the Mac. It helps you write, express yourself, and get things done based on your personal context, making everything simpler and easier. The new iMac is ready for it.

### Performance that looks as good as it is

The M4 chip brings a 10-core CPU (four performance cores and six efficiency cores), a 10-core GPU, and a 16-core Neural Engine — a striking jump in both performance and AI. The advanced GPU supports hardware-accelerated ray tracing, so images look more alive.

### A display that is hard to miss

The iMac display is still the centre of attention. It is a 24-inch Retina display with 4.5K resolution and 500 nits of brightness, so detail holds up. P3 wide colour makes everything more vivid.

### Camera, mics, speakers: all eyes on you

The 12MP Center Stage camera keeps you centred. Desk View shows your workspace while you are on camera. Studio-quality mics make your voice clearer, and the six-speaker system with Spatial Audio and Dolby Atmos makes what you hear feel big and real.
