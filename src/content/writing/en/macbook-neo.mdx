Apple has released a MacBook powered by the A18 Pro — the MacBook Neo. As MKBHD said, I also assumed at first that Neo was just an internal codename, but it turns out that is really what it is called.

The MacBook Neo comes in four colours: silver, peach pink, citrus yellow, and indigo.

It starts at ¥4,599, which makes it Apple's cheapest entry-level MacBook. The cheapest MacBook used to be the MacBook Air, but that now costs nearly twice as much as the new MacBook Neo. The 256GB model has no Touch ID; the model starting at ¥5,299 does.

There are two speakers at each end of the chassis. One end also has a USB 3 port, a USB 2 port, and a 3.5 mm headphone jack. According to MKBHD, the trackpad is an old-style clickable trackpad, like the MacBooks of years ago. The initial resolution is 2408 × 1506, with a peak brightness of 500 nits.

The MacBook Neo is Apple's attempt at the low-end computer market, and it should make a MacBook more appealing to people who had held off because of the price.
