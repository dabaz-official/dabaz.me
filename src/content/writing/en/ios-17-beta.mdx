## Messages

### Voice message transcription

As shown here, a voice message I sent can now be turned into text. In testing, transcription was extremely fast: it was already text by the time the message went out.

<PostFigure
  src={figure2}
  alt="A voice message in Messages with its automatic transcript below"
  caption="Auto Transcription"
/>

### The plus button beside the input field

Tap the plus button beside the message field and a new menu opens. It holds some of the iPhone's built-in Messages features; tap "More" for third-party apps.

<PostFigure
  src={figure3}
  alt="The Messages menu opened from the plus button"
/>

### Check In

I carry a China-region iPhone, so Check In is not in that menu. The first-run copy for Check In reads "Let your friend know you've arrived with Check In." In short, when you reach the destination you set, Check In sends a message to your friend automatically. After tapping continue, you can choose to share all of your information or only part of it.

### Stickers

The menu includes Stickers. With this new feature you can cut the subject out of any photo in your library and turn it into a sticker.

## AirTag sharing

You can now share an AirTag with someone else. Once shared, they can locate it too.

## Interactive widgets

iOS 17 adds interactive widgets. You can, for example, play a playlist directly from the Apple Music widget.

## Music

### SharePlay in cars

SharePlay now works in cars with CarPlay.

### Crossfade

If you use Spotify often, this one will be familiar. Crossfade blends two songs smoothly and naturally.

## StandBy

StandBy is a genuinely useful new feature. Put the iPhone in landscape on a charger and StandBy appears. It can show a simple clock, the song now playing, or two widgets side by side.

## AutoFill

In iOS 17, verification codes that arrive by email can be filled in automatically.

## Siri

You can now summon Siri with just "Siri". In Settings you can choose "'Siri' or 'Hey Siri'", "'Hey Siri'", or "Off".

## Safari

### Switching tab groups

You can now swipe left and right to switch between Private Browsing and other tab groups.

### Safer Private Browsing

Private Browsing now requires Face ID or Touch ID to unlock. Tracking management is stricter, and so is extension management. Safari in Settings has more options.

<PostFigure
  src={figure11}
  alt="Private Browsing locked behind Face ID"
/>

### Profiles

Safari now supports separate profiles, much like profiles in Google Chrome.

## Shared passwords

Passwords can now be shared with your family.

<PostFigure
  src={figure12}
  alt="A list of shared family passwords"
  caption="Family Passwords"
/>

## NameDrop

NameDrop changes how iPhone users exchange contact details. Bring two iPhones together, or an iPhone and an Apple Watch, and the information is shared.

## AirDrop that continues

Even if you and your friend walk away from each other, AirDrop can keep transferring over the network. This feature has not shipped yet.

## Live Voicemail

When you miss a call, the voicemail the caller leaves is transcribed in real time on your iPhone.
